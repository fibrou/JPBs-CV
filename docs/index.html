About

John Paul Broussard is a Finance Professor with over 25 years of experience spanning academic administration, international program development, applied financial research, and classroom instruction.

His administrative record includes directing and co-directing graduate finance programs at the University of Oklahoma — including the Online MS Finance Program (150+ students, graduation rate above 78%, CFA Institute University Affiliation) — and serving multiple terms as Finance Area Head at Rutgers University and the Estonian Business School. He has led curriculum redesign, faculty recruitment, strategic planning, and development office engagement at institutions across three continents.

His research applies advanced quantitative methods to empirical finance. Current work employs vine copula modeling, multimodal AI and NLP, and network analysis to study central bank communication, endowment portfolio management, commodity market dependence, financial influencer effects, and the interaction of alternative and GAAP earnings. His work has appeared in Journal of Financial Economics, Management Science, European Financial Management, and 20 other peer-reviewed outlets.

His international portfolio spans sustained academic engagement in the United States, Estonia, Finland, England, Switzerland, and Italy, including a Fulbright Distinguished Chair in Business and Economics (Hanken School, 2016) and a Fulbright Finland Alumni Grant (2026).

His teaching integrates professional market standards with coursework. As a CFA Charterholder, Certified Financial Risk Manager, and Professional Risk Manager — and a trainer for both designation programs for nearly two decades — he brings practitioner depth to every course, across in-person, online, and international delivery formats.
